# Full-body alternative routine: research and plan

Status: **v2 built, seeded and active** (21 Sep 2026). The 6-day split is retained as the "Split (6-day)" library entry Dave can switch back to; the full-body routine below is the active one. §1 and §2 are the original research; §3 and §4 describe the routine as built.

## 1. Why: what the data says

Over the last 10 ISO weeks (W28–W38) Dave completed a mean of ~2.8 sessions/week against a 6/week plan (~47% adherence); over the last 8 weeks it is 3.5/week (4, 5, 2, 3, 6, 2, 3, 3). Only one week (W35) hit all six.

Most-missed sessions:

| Session | Times missed |
|---|---|
| Pull + Legs (the long combined day) | 6 |
| Parkrun + core | 4 |
| Pull (back & arms) | 3 |
| Push (chest & arms) | 3 |
| Push (shoulders) + treadmill run | 3 |

Because the split hits each muscle group on one specific day, a single miss removes that group for the week, and a repeat miss stretches to two weeks. The daily rehab block has not been ticked since 1 Sep 2026.

**Conclusion:** 3–4 full-body sessions/week matches what Dave actually does. The alternative should be built around observed behaviour, not aspiration.

## 2. Evidence summary

- **Frequency doesn't matter when weekly volume is equal.** Schoenfeld, Grgic & Krieger 2019 (25 studies): no meaningful hypertrophy difference by frequency at equated volume. Ramos-Campo et al. 2024 (14 studies): full-body vs split, no difference in strength or muscle size. Pelland et al. 2024/25 meta-regression: frequency has a small positive effect on *strength*, negligible on size. So full-body 3x loses nothing and may gain a little strength.
- **Volume target:** ~10–20 hard sets per muscle per week; minimum effective ~4 (Iversen 2021). Three full-body sessions × 3–4 sets per muscle = 9–12 sets, squarely in range.
- **Adherence:** Conti et al. 2026 (389k app users): early consistency is the strongest predictor of still training at 12 months; "achievable" beats "optimal". No RCT tests full-body vs split for adherence. The robustness argument (a missed session costs every muscle a third of its week rather than wiping out a body part) is reasoned, not measured.
- **Session length:** 6–8 exercises × 3 sets runs 60–80 min with straight sets. Antagonist paired sets (push/pull, curl/triceps) cut time ~35% at equal gains (2025 Sports Med meta; Iversen 2021). Rest ~90 s upper, ~2 min lower is enough (Singer 2024).
- **Lower back:** keep squatting and hinging but regress: goblet/belt/leg press over barbell back squat; chest-supported rows over bent-over; RDL from light before any floor pull; trap-bar deadlift if progressing to heavy hinges. McGill Big 3 (curl-up, side plank, bird dog) daily is as effective as other active rehab; 10–15 min daily home work reduces symptoms.
- **Fatigue on consecutive full-body days:** rotate lower-body heavy/moderate/light across A/B/C (undulating is free for hypertrophy, slightly better for strength). Scheduled deloads not required; deload reactively when back, sleep or performance slips.
- **4 days:** full-body 4x and upper/lower are equivalent at equal volume. For someone who misses sessions, full-body 4x is more robust, but keep only one heavy lower day to spare the back.

### The LessWrong post

Most likely the one Dave remembers: **samusasuke, "Why you maybe should lift weights, and How to." (12 Feb 2025)** — https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Sz6xDcS9JHdwMgXPz/why-you-maybe-should-lift-weights-and-how-to. Prescription: 2–3 × 45 min full-body sessions/week, A/B alternating, everything in antagonist supersets, 3 working sets, 5–20 reps close to failure, double progression (+10% load when you exceed the rep range), 48–96 h between sessions, "ignore anything fancy". It asserts higher frequency is better but cites no studies.

Also relevant: RomeoStevens' "Optimal Exercise" (2014), its 2023 reflections (spine-friendly swaps: front squat/step-ups over back squat, RDLs, "the dis-utility from injuries vastly outweighs positive effects from chasing numbers") and "Time Efficient Resistance Training" (2024: goblet squat ⇄ RDL, incline press ⇄ one-arm row, 3 × 8–20, 1 min rests).

## 3. Proposed routine (v2, finalised 21 Sep 2026)

### Weekly shape

| Day | Session | Venue | ~Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Home core + mobility** (the 15-min block) + 5-a-side football in the evening | home, pitch | 15 min + match |
| Tue | **Full body A** (push + pull + legs) + treadmill run AFTER the lifts | gym | 55–65 min + run |
| Wed | **Home core + mobility** | home | 15 min |
| Thu | **Full body B** (push + pull + legs) + treadmill run AFTER the lifts | gym | 55–65 min + run |
| Fri | **Home core + mobility** | home | 15 min |
| Sat | **Parkrun** first, then **Full body C** (push + pull + legs) | outdoor, gym | 5 km + 45–55 min |
| Sun | **Home core + mobility** (recovery) + long walk + yoga | home | 15 min + walk |

Three full-body gym sessions (Tue/Thu/Sat), each spaced so every muscle is hit within a few days if one is missed. The 15-minute home core + mobility block runs on the four non-gym days as a FIXED prescription: no AI programming, no accessories, no progression — the day's checklist is exactly the block below. Runs stay on their own effort: Tue/Thu treadmill AFTER the lifts, Sat parkrun FIRST. Monday keeps 5-a-side football in the evening; Sunday adds a long walk + yoga (the block is recovery, not training).

### Gym sessions

Four antagonist PAIRS plus calves. All working sets 8–12 reps unless stated; calves and carries 12–15; three working sets throughout. Antagonist pairs are done back-to-back: 30–60 s between the two halves, ~90 s before the next round. Two light warm-up sets on the first movement of each pair. Double progression: top of the range on all three sets → add weight and reset reps; below the bottom → drop about 10%. Alternatives (shown as "or …" on the checklist) let a machine be swapped when it is taken. Exercise names are the exact history spellings where one exists; **(new)** movements have no history yet.

**A — Tuesday**
1. Leg press ⇄ Seated leg curl — plus Calf press **(new)** on the leg press, straight after each leg-press set (12–15)
2. Incline DB press ⇄ Chest-supported DB row (or Single-arm DB row **(new)**)
3. Neutral-grip pull-up (or Neutral-grip lat pulldown) ⇄ Hanging knee raise
4. DB bicep curl (or Hammer curls) ⇄ Overhead DB tricep extension
Then the treadmill run (easy pace, distance ramping toward the 10k goal).

**B — Thursday**
1. Converging chest press ⇄ Seated cable row
2. Converging shoulder press ⇄ Wide-grip lat pulldown (or Diverging lat pulldown)
3. Leg extension ⇄ Back extension **(new)** (or DB good morning **(new)**)
4. Machine bicep curl **(new)** (or Hammer curls) ⇄ Machine tricep extension **(new)** (or DB skull crusher **(new)**)
Then Seated calf raise **(new)**, straight sets 3 × 12–15, then the treadmill run.

**C — Saturday, after parkrun**
0. Parkrun 5 km FIRST.
1. DB Romanian deadlift **(new)** ⇄ DB lateral raise
2. Flat DB press ⇄ Single-arm DB row **(new)** (or Chest-supported DB row)
3. Bulgarian split squat (or Reverse lunge) ⇄ Incline dumbbell curls (or Hammer curls)
4. Overhead DB tricep extension ⇄ Standing calf raise (step)

Every session ends on one accessory taken to failure. Calf work is gym-days-only; the home block is mobility and core, not calves.

## 4. Daily home core + mobility block

The 15-minute block, done in this order on Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun as the day's FIXED prescription (no AI, no accessories, no progression — the checklist is exactly this list with these doses). It replaces the old separate rehab panel: the `rehabRoutine` data domain is retained and still readable, but its standalone UI and API were removed.

| Exercise | Dose | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Cat-cow | 8 slow | warm the spine |
| Couch stretch | 90 s per side | front of the hip, not the lower back |
| 90/90 hip switch | 8 per side | hip rotation, chest up |
| McGill curl-up | 3-2-1 × 10 s holds | brace, keep the lower back still |
| Side plank | 3-2-1 × 10 s per side | on knees if the full version aggravates |
| Bird dog | 3-2-1 × 10 s per side | hips level |
| Glute bridge | 2 × 15, 2 s squeeze | ribs down, don't arch |
| Standing pelvic tilt | 10 | "run tall, tuck the pelvis" |

Sunday is the same block as recovery, alongside a long walk and yoga; Monday adds 5-a-side football in the evening. Under 15 minutes — the LBP adherence literature says short and simple wins.


## 5. Implementation plan (DONE — v2 built and seeded 21 Sep 2026)

The routine is data: one `user_data` row, domain `weeklyRoutine`, a JSON array of seven `WeeklyRoutineDay` objects. There is no library/active-routine concept today.

1. **Snapshot** the live `weeklyRoutine` JSON to a file before anything else (`sqlite3 ~/.claude/data/portal/calendar.db "SELECT data FROM user_data WHERE domain='weeklyRoutine'"`). Never overwrite it un-snapshotted.
2. **Add a routine library** (small, additive):
   - New domains `weeklyRoutineLibrary: Record<string, WeeklyRoutineDay[]>` and `activeRoutineName: string`, whitelisted in `src/lib/storage/core.ts` (~line 175).
   - `getWeeklyRoutine()` in `src/lib/storage/weekly-routine.ts:117` resolves `library[active]`, falling back to the existing `weeklyRoutine` row (which becomes the "Split (6-day)" entry). No caller signatures change; the routine fingerprint already folds title/note/anchors/staples into the programme hash, so switching regenerates programmes automatically.
   - API: `GET/PUT /api/exercise/routine` gains `?name=`; new `POST /api/exercise/routine/activate { name }` that calls `prewarmProgramme()`.
   - UI: routine picker at the top of `RoutineTab.tsx`; "Duplicate as…" to create the full-body entry from the current one.
3. **Seed the "Full body (3+1)" routine** with the seven days from §3. Titles must contain parser-recognised group words (`push`, `pull`, `legs`, `core`, `run`) — plain "Full body" activates no muscle groups and weakens the programmer. Use e.g. `Full body A (push + pull + legs)`. Anchors per day = the compounds listed; staples = the core finisher.
4. **Programmer tweaks** (`src/lib/exercise-programmer.ts`): add a prompt line for full-body days asking for antagonist pairing and a ~60-min budget, and allow 6–8 rows rather than "anchors + 3–5 accessories" (check `guaranteeGroupCoverage` still passes with three groups on one day). Add a test in `exercise-programmer.test.ts` for a three-component day.
5. **Extend the rehab list** via the existing `PUT /api/exercise/rehab` (no code change) with the §4 movements and McGill doses.
6. **Materialiser**: 14-day horizon reconciles future routine-sourced plans automatically; past sessions untouched. Verify the Google Calendar mirror re-dates cleanly.
7. **Tests to keep green**: `weekly-routine-storage.test.ts` pins `SEED_ROUTINE`, so do not touch the seed; new tests for library resolution and activation.
8. **Measure**: after 4 weeks compare sessions/week and per-muscle weekly sets (via `exercise-analysis.ts`) against the W31–W38 baseline of 3.5/week. Success = ≥3 sessions/week in 3 of 4 weeks and every muscle group hit ≥2×/week.

Estimated effort: half a day for the library + picker, an hour for the seed and rehab list, an hour for programmer prompt + tests.

## 6. Decisions (Dave, 20 Sep 2026)

1. Cardio unchanged: Tue and Thu treadmill runs, Sat parkrun, Mon football. Runs on separate days from weights. Running goals remain active.
2. Lifting therefore lands on Mon/Wed/Fri. Monday is a double with football; see the note under Weekly shape.
3. Back: don't over-engineer. Strength and mobility are what improve it and it is much better than it was. Dumbbell RDL goes in and progresses normally.
4. No fourth lifting day.
